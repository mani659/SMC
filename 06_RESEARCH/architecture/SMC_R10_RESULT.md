Why R10 was required:

  The programme needed to explicitly separate "positive expectancy"

Economic qualification hierarchy:
  Level 1: Scientific Effect — evidence of predictive information
  Level 2: Economic Candidate — positive net expectancy after costs (M3)
  Level 3: Validated Module — survives stronger validation (M4)
  Level 4: Deployment Candidate — suitable for bot inclusion

Minimum economic threshold:
  E[R_net] > 0 under frozen methodology
  No arbitrary universal minimum (bps, dollars, %)

Rare-event policy:
  Rare events can qualify if evidence is sufficient
  Distinguish frequency, evidence sufficiency, and attractiveness
  Do not impose arbitrary minimum event counts

Scientific effect threshold:
  Any credible evidence of information content

Economic candidate threshold:
  Positive net expectancy after realistic costs

Validated module threshold:
  Survives OOS, dependence treatment, execution realism

Deployment threshold:
  Capital efficiency, risk, capacity, execution burden

Standalone strategy definition:
  Complete trade-generating system, active continuously

Specialist module definition:
  Specific economic role, defined activation condition, restricted domain

Conditioning module definition:
  Validated evidence that it changes behavior of another generator
  Requires independent justification, not PnL improvement

Anti-combination-mining rule:
  Test every combination → pick best = FORBIDDEN
  Each module must qualify independently

Module independence rule:
  Each module must have standalone positive expectancy
  Cannot depend on other modules for own validity

Interaction rule:
  1. Both modules independently validated (M4)
  2. Complementary economic roles
  3. Interaction defined ex ante
  4. Combined test = new hypothesis
  5. Combined OOS untouched

Economic magnitude principle:
  Small positive expectancy is still positive
  Do not invent universal minimum edge
  Economically positive ≠ economically scalable

Capital/scalability principle:
  Deployment layer handles scale
  Not a requirement for proving positive expectancy

Risk/execution principle:
  Deployment dimensions: drawdown, variance, tail risk, capacity
  NOT additional requirements for Level 2

Failed artifact treatment:
  Scientifically informative, economically non-viable
  NOT automatically a filter/confirmation/regime label
  May inform genuinely new future hypotheses
