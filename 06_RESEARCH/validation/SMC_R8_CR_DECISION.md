## Key Finding: Causal Integrity Preserved

The critical audit found that R8's event chain IS causally identifiable — but only after the wording clarifications. The core issue was:

> "Final extreme of the trend" and "last HL/LH in the trend" could be interpreted as requiring hindsight.

The amendments fix this by anchoring both the sweep level and CHOCH swing to the sweep timestamp, not the trend endpoint.
