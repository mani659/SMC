## What This Decision Protects

The programme has established a genuine scientific finding:

> SMC structural definitions detect real but tiny price effects on XAUUSD M1. These effects are below transaction-cost requirements at the observed signal frequency.

This finding is valuable. It separates:
- **Signal existence** (real, tiny, positive gross effects)
- **Economic viability** (non-viable under realistic costs)

Without the control architecture, the programme might have:
- Combined failing models into a "strategy"
- Optimized parameters until something looked profitable
- Claimed success based on in-sample overfitting

The control architecture prevented all of these.
