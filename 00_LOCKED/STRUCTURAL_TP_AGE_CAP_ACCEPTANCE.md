## 3. Why `none` was rejected and `age100` was not approved

These are the two distinct failure modes, and they are not the same failure.

1. **`none` fails on pre-registered grounds and on target quality.** It is over the K2 line (78.2 % of newly-selected swings older than 24 h; 57 older than 7 days; oldest 61 days). Independently of that, the *quality* of what it selects is degenerate: recovered `|TP − entry|` **p50 0.274 ATR** with a 0.257–0.359 p25–p75 band pressed against the `0.25 × ATR` noise guard. With no cap, "nearest in price" is free to choose the oldest level sitting a hair above entry — a structural branch whose median objective is 0.27 ATR is worse than the 4×ATR fallback it would replace.
2. **`age100` survives on quality but is not a coverage fix.** Its recovered targets are genuine structure (p50 **0.585 ATR**, p75 1.07). But it re-prices **105 of the 218** existing structural fills (48.17 %, over the K3 line) — the book is not "92 more structural targets", it is *92 gained and 105 changed*: 197 of 351 fills (56 %) see a different plan. Adopting that is a **selection change** decision, not a window-tightness decision, and one probe on one book is not that decision.
3. **`b1` proves only where the loss is.** Cap at `ceil(20 × 5 / tf_minutes)` leaves M5 at 20 and *tightens* M15 (7) and H1 (2), so its 48.12 % recovery is entirely M1. It is reported as sensitivity and is not a reading of C.

**The load-bearing sentence:** the same +69 % recovery looks like a repair under a 100-bar window and like an artifact with no cap — which is why the ruling separates "widen" from "what got selected".

## 4. Method notes retained (bind any future re-use)

### 4.1 Prefix detection is required — full-series detection is look-ahead

Swing detection must run on `series[: idx + 1]` at the placement bar. A swing whose extreme sits 1–2 bars before placement can be confirmed before it yet be **invisible at place time**, because its right-hand confirmation window (`N_BAR_LTF = 3`) is not complete. Demonstrated: `F_feb25_L_2025-02-25_poi-015315` gives **2953.348** full-series vs **2944.648** prefix at a 20-bar cap. The engine has the same blind spot, so prefix detection is the faithful method; any harness that drops the bound produces targets the engine cannot see and is invalid for this question.

### 4.2 The ranking `(dist, -candle_index)` explains both the churn and the 0-pathology

`structural_tp_target` ranks candidates by **distance first**, recency only as a tie-break. Therefore an age change does not merely filter — it can change *which* swing wins:

- **Churn:** any loosening lets an older, nearer level displace a recent, farther one → 48 % / 94 % / 38 % of the existing structural book changes level.
- **`none`'s 0.25 ATR pile-up:** with no cap, the nearest-above-entry level wins regardless of age, so recovered targets cluster on the noise guard (p50 0.274 ATR).

Any future measurement that reports only "share structural" or "recovery %" is measuring coverage and is incomplete by construction.

## 5. Requirements for any future C lock

All four must be true before an implementation is drafted. Failing any one of them means the proposal repeats a defect this engagement already proved.

1. **Named constant with an explicit unit.** A `STRUCTURAL_TP_MAX_AGE_BARS`-style name is **not sufficient alone** — the unit must be stated in the definition (confirmation-TF bars on the menu path, with the TF the bars are counted on stated explicitly; the seek path is M5 by construction). A bare integer for all TFs is the original defect and cannot be locked.
2. **Finite age.** `None` is disqualified: K2 fail and the 0.274 ATR target quality. No unbounded window.
3. **Either accept and re-read the churn set, or change the rank rule in the same design.** A window widening that leaves `(dist, -candle_index)` untouched must name the fills it re-prices (the churn set) and re-read them; the alternative is to rule the ranking itself in the same note. Shipping a wider window and reporting only recovery is not acceptable.
4. **Book-scoped measurement first; no product default flip from a single probe.** Any candidate is measured on Book F (P0 scope) under a pre-registered contract before it is considered for any other book or any default. One probe is not a lock.

**Also required by existing discipline (unchanged by this note):** definition document → Architect accept → measure; `LOCKED_DECISIONS`-style formal editing only after a dated ruling; no threshold or trigger movement smuggled in with the window.
