Base Candle Definition
Bullish trend: Base Candle = candle whose HIGH = swing high
Bearish trend: Base Candle = candle whose LOW = swing low
Bearish exception: If next candle's wick creates lower low but current candle is bearish, the bearish candle is still the Base Candle.
Swing Validity Decision Gate
1. Did price break Base Candle's OPPOSITE extreme?
   ✗ NO → SWING = INVALID
   ✓ YES → Continue

2. Did candle CLOSE beyond that extreme?
   ✗ Wick only → SWING = INVALID
   ✓ Body close → SWING = VALID → Proceed
⚠️ Two-bar reversal alone does NOT confirm swing validity. Base Candle extreme MUST be broken and closed beyond.

📋 Formal Demand & Supply Zone Definition (Expert — Frozen)
Demand Zone: The single last bearish candle that appears just before a strong bullish impulsive move. Zone drawn from high to low of that single opposing candle (full body + wicks). NOT the bullish candles — the demand zone is the last opposing candle before the impulse.

Supply Zone: The single last bullish candle that appears just before a strong bearish impulsive move. Zone drawn from high to low of that single opposing candle (full body + wicks). NOT the bearish candles — the supply zone is the last opposing candle before the impulse.

Freshness: 1-touch only (same as all models). Demand Evidence: 650 pip and 2400 pip moves from Daily demand zones. Supply Evidence: H4 single zones, multiple zones at different levels, and retracement examples.
